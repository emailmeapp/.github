# Email Me: Audio Notes

**Email yourself notes in one tap.** Type a note or record a voice memo, let AI transcribe it, and get it straight in your inbox.

[Website](https://emailmeapp.net) · [Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/audio-notes-email-me/id1090744587) · [Blog](https://emailmeapp.net/blog/) · [Support](https://emailmeapp.net/help/)

## What it does

- Send a note to your own email in one tap
- Record voice notes and get an AI transcription in the email
- Works on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch
- Free to download
